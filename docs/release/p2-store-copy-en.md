## Full description

**ZOUBEACON (小象避坑)** — a research companion that organizes property information and flags what to verify before you buy or rent in Japan.

**How it helps**
- 📷 Snap photos or upload documents; ZOUBEACON structures the key facts: property type, size, address, land right and more
- 🔍 Free preview: a completeness score per dimension plus deterministic, statutory-capped estimates of acquisition costs (brokerage, stamp duty, registration tax and similar)
- 📊 Market comparables: same-ward condominium sold-price references (1LDK/2LDK/3LDK) from the Japanese MLIT transaction-price information system (source attributed)
- 📄 Deep report: sold-price reference details, FX notes and data-caliber statements; one purchase unlocks all reports for your account
- 💾 Your queries and documents stay in your account for later review

**Data & compliance notes**
- Sold-price references come from MLIT (国土交通省) 土地総合情報システム transaction data — open government data, source attributed
- CNY/USD conversions in reports are display-only; rate source and date are noted in each report
- The free preview and deep report are not a full appraisal, tax advice or legal opinion; confirm critical decisions with qualified professionals
- No unauthorized scraping: data sources without a license (e.g. rental market feeds) are not displayed until authorized
